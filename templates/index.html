🏗️ Ghost Project Detector

Catch fake and poor-quality government infrastructure projects

The Problem

Government releases crores for roads, wells, and buildings in villages. Contractors take the money but don't build anything — or build it poorly. Projects show as "completed" on paper but don't exist on the ground.

Our Solution

An independent verification platform that compares government claims against physical, geographic, photographic, financial and temporal evidence.
